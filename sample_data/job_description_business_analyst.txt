Job Title: IT Business Analyst / Scrum Master

Company: FinTech Solutions Group

About the Role:
We are looking for a skilled IT Business Analyst to bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical engineering teams.

Responsibilities:
- Gather, analyze, and document business and functional requirements (BRDs, FRDs, User Stories)
- Facilitate Scrum ceremonies including Sprint Planning, Daily Standups, and Sprint Retrospectives
- Conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT), gap analysis, and workflow process mapping
- Collaborate with developers and QA engineers to ensure delivered software meets client expectations

Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience as an IT Business Analyst or Systems Analyst in Banking or Healthcare
- Certified Scrum Master (CSM) or proven track record in Agile / Scrum methodologies
- Proficiency in Jira, Confluence, Visio, and SQL queries for data verification
- Exceptional stakeholder communication and team leadership skills
